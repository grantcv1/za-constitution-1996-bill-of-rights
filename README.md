# The Constitution of South Africa, 1996 in Akoma Ntoso

The Preamble and Chapter 2 (the Bill of Rights, sections 7 to 39) of the Constitution of the Republic of South Africa, 1996, in Akoma Ntoso XML, with a stylesheet and a page that set it line for line as first gazetted. It was generated by Claude for Grant Vergottini.

This is an extract, in English only, and it is the text as published on December 18, 1996, not the Constitution as later amended. It is not an official version.

## About the markup

- **Standard:** Akoma Ntoso 2.0 (AKN 3.1). The namespace is `http://docs.oasis-open.org/legaldocml/ns/akn/3.0`, with `version="3.1"` on the root element.
- **Validation:** The XML validates against the Akoma Ntoso 2.0 cs01 schema.
- **Structure:** An `<act>` holds the Preamble in `<preamble>`, then a `<chapter>` with `<section>`, `<subsection>`, `<list>` and `<point>` elements. Sections use the South African terms, and side headings are `<heading>` elements.
- **Table:** The Table of Non-Derogable Rights in section 37 is a `<table>` in an `<hcontainer>`.
- **Line and page breaks:** `<eol>` and `<eop>` record each printed line and page end.
- **Identifiers:** Every element with an `eId` also has a `GUID`. Text-level `<p>`, `<intro>` and `<content>` elements have neither.
- **Cross-references:** References to sections and subsections in the Chapter are `<ref>` elements. Those inside the table, and paragraph references, are plain text.

## About the rendition

`view.html` rebuilds Gazette pages 3 and 7 to 19 from the XML. The running heads are on the printed pages but aren't part of the Act, so the page adds them and the XML leaves them out. The typefaces are Source Serif 4, Nunito Sans and Barlow Condensed, chosen to resemble the printing. They aren't the original type, so this is not a facsimile.

## How the text was made

The PDF is a scan with an OCR layer full of errors, such as `Mrica` for `Africa` and `thanil days` for `than 21 days`. Claude corrected the 521 lines by hand and compared them line by line with the OCR. It looked at only part of the scan images directly, including the Preamble, the first Chapter 2 page, the table and section 28(2). A full proofread against the scan or a later official text has not been done, so small errors may remain.

The section numbers and the table were keyed from the scan. The table is not split into printed lines.

## Later changes

The Constitution has been amended since 1996, so some of these provisions may differ today. For the current text, see the official sources.
